Before you book

Good to know

  • Arrive Friday 4 Dec and set up; depart Sunday 6 Dec. Flights Warsaw-Berlin are excluded from the budget.
  • Airport transfer: Airport Express or S-Bahn with an ABC ticket (about 5 EUR per person each way); local transport for the group is estimated at about 130 EUR.
  • SDP also plays Uber Arena on Fri 4 Dec and Eisbären play there on Sun 6 Dec, so expect busy trains and hotels around Ostbahnhof.
  • Christmas markets are open in this period, which may raise hotel prices.
  • Leave at least 3 hours before the return flight on Sunday.
  • Nothing is booked; use the contacts on each option to book yourself.

Check when you call

  • Union v HSV kick-off is 15:30 on a suggested date per the club site (afternoon, not the Saturday evening you asked for).
  • The Union price is a resale estimate from a third-party page, not face value.
  • The SDP price is a 'from' price, so the real group total can be higher.
  • Hotel rates are indicative figures from other dates; check live rates for 4-6 Dec 2026 and confirm twin rooms.
  • Hampton phone and e-mail and Goldmarie phone are unconfirmed.
  • Weather is climatology only; re-check the forecast from about 18 Nov 2026.
  • Ticketmaster data was unavailable (API key rejected); options come from web sources.
  • Konzerthaus programme and start time are not published; confirm before choosing.
