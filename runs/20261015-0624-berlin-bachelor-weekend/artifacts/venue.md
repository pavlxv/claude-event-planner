# Venue shortlist: Berlin bachelor weekend hubs

## Summary
For this bachelor weekend the venue is the main hub of the Saturday-evening action. Three Berlin hubs are shortlisted: the Uber Platz entertainment district (arena plus music hall, bars and restaurants, two hotels on the square), the Union Berlin stadium in Köpenick, and the Olympiastadion. Visiting an area costs nothing; ticket prices belong to the live-events shortlist. Uber Platz is best because a concert is listed there on 4-5 Dec 2026 and it is the most compact base.
