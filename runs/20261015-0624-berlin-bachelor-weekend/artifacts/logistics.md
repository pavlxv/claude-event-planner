## Summary
The 8 guests fly Warsaw to Berlin BER on Fri 4 Dec and return on Sun 6 Dec (flights are excluded from the budget). Because weather risk is high, the airport transfer uses covered rail (Airport Express FEX or S-Bahn, ABC ticket, about 5 EUR each way) and local trips use U-Bahn/S-Bahn or a taxi instead of walking. Three hotels in the Friedrichshain hub next to Uber Platz are shortlisted for 4 rooms x 2 nights, each about 900-1,000 EUR. Hotel prices are indicative figures from booking-site listings, not quotes for these dates, so the user must check live rates.

Local-transfer plan:
- BER is in fare zone C, so guests need an ABC ticket; an AB ticket is not enough. One ABC single is 5.00 EUR and is valid 2 hours including transfers, and the FEX needs no extra supplement.
- Fri 4 Dec (min about -0.5 C): take the covered FEX/S-Bahn from BER and, if the hotel is far from the station, a U-Bahn hop or taxi (about 60-70 EUR for the whole airport ride, split among 8 it needs 2 taxis; rail is cheaper).
- Sat 5 Dec: the hotels sit 5-10 min on foot or one stop from Uber Platz; use the ABC 24-hour group ticket (33.50 EUR for up to 5 people, so 2 tickets for 8 = 67 EUR) or the U1/S-Bahn from Warschauer Straße for the concert; late return by night bus or taxi.
- Sun 6 Dec: ABC singles back to BER, leaving at least 3 hours before the flight.
- Crowds: SDP also plays Uber Arena on Fri 4 Dec 19:30 and Eisbären play there Sun 6 Dec 16:30, so expect busy trains and hotels around Ostbahnhof/Warschauer Straße on arrival and departure days. The Union Berlin match (Köpenick, Sat 5 Dec) has a kick-off time still to be determined, so no transfer to the Alte Försterei stadium is planned until it is confirmed; the hub stays Uber Arena / Friedrichshain.
- Estimated transport for the group: 16 ABC singles = 80 EUR, plus local tickets about 30-70 EUR. Total roughly 110-150 EUR, which fits with the hotel cost inside 3,500 EUR (it is shared with the show and dinner budgets, so the schedule must account for it).
